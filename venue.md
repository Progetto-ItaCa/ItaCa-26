### How to get to Florence 
 
Florence is the capital of Tuscany, in central Italy. You can easily get there by plane, train or bus.

If you travel by plane, the two most convenient airports are:

- **Florence Amerigo Vespucci Airport (FLR)**, connected to the city centre by tram line T2.
- **Pisa Galileo Galilei Airport (PSA)**, connected to Florence by the PisaMover shuttle followed by a train, or by direct coach services.

If you travel by train, the main railway station is **[Firenze Santa Maria Novella (SMN)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KqGes2aTZYrhGtaUA)**. Another convenient station is **[Firenze Rifredi](https://maps.app.goo.gl/dz3iN4M1ptq2raXn7)**, which is the closest railway station to the venue.

If you travel by bus, you can arrive at **[Villa Costanza](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zHcJe4vYSFf6uC3f7)**, a major long-distance coach stop, directly connected to the city centre by tram line T1.

 
### How to get to the venue

The closest tram stop is [Morgagni–Università](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5oAhFg3dk1LdFtWP8), just a short walk from the entrance. The nearest bus stops are [Morgagni Careggi](https://maps.app.goo.gl/1dB9CooETqUQkMW47) and [Santo Stefano In Pane](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DjtfHzVfEWLwppCt6).


- From Florence Amerigo Vespucci Airport: take tram line T2 from Peretola Aeroporto towards the city centre and get off at [Alamanni–Stazione](https://maps.app.goo.gl/UU3JKarFoPYuNyEG6). Change to tram line T1 towards Careggi–Ospedale and get off at [Morgagni–Università](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5oAhFg3dk1LdFtWP8). 
- From Pisa Galileo Galilei Airport: take the [PisaMover](https://pisa-mover.com/en/) to Pisa Centrale and then a train to Firenze Santa Maria Novella. From there, take tram line T1 towards Careggi–Ospedale and get off at [Morgagni–Università](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5oAhFg3dk1LdFtWP8).
- From Firenze Santa Maria Novella: take tram line T1 towards Careggi–Ospedale and get off at [Morgagni–Università](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5oAhFg3dk1LdFtWP8). The journey takes about 20 minutes.
- From Firenze Rifredi: the venue can be reached on foot in approximately 10–15 minutes.
- From Firenze Campo di Marte: take a local train to Firenze Santa Maria Novella and then tram line T1 towards Careggi–Ospedale, getting off at [Morgagni–Università](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5oAhFg3dk1LdFtWP8).
- From Villa Costanza: take tram line T1 towards Careggi–Ospedale and get off directly at [Morgagni–Università](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5oAhFg3dk1LdFtWP8). The journey takes about 40 minutes.


###  Practical information

A standard urban ticket in Florence is valid for 90 minutes and can be used on buses and trams. The current fare is €2. Tickets can be bought from ticket machines, through the at bus app, or directly on board using a contactless credit/debit card.

Another convenient way of getting around Florence is by bike. The city has an extensive network of cycle lanes and a bike-sharing service operated by [RideMovi](https://www.ridemovi.com/it/).
