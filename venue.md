### How to get to Florence 
 
Florence is the capital of Tuscany, in central Italy. You can easily get there by plane, train or bus.

If you travel by plane, the two most convenient airports are:
- Florence Airport, Peretola (FLR), formally "Amerigo Vespucci" Airport (FLR), which is very close to the city centre and directly connected to it by tram line T2 reaching central Florence in about 20 minutes;
- Pisa Galileo Galilei Airport (PSA), which has convenient connections to Florence by train and direct shuttle bus. 

If you travel by train, the main railway station is Firenze Santa Maria Novella (SMN), in the city centre. Another important station is Firenze Campo di Marte,  served by several regional and long-distance trains.
 
If you travel by bus, you can arrive at the main bus station, Villa Costanza, directly connected to the city centre by tram line T1.
